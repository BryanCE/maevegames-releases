# Changelog

## 0.0.1

The first release of Maeve's Games.

### Games

- **Who's playing?** Each player has a picture, a color and a star total. Progress is saved per player and carries over between updates.
- **Math**, following Ray's New Primary Arithmetic, with four games:
  - **Count the balls**: touch each ball to count it, then pick how many there are.
  - **How many birds?**: birds land on a branch; count them and pick the number.
  - **Apple basket**: put the asked-for number of apples in the basket, then touch the check.
  - **One more bird**: count the birds, watch one arrive or fly away, and say how many there are now.
- Every game asks **how high to count** before it starts: up to 5, 10 or 20, and up to 100 for counting balls. Each size remembers its own level, and the numbers grow as she gets them right.
- Every game starts with a short spoken **how-to-play**.

### Counting and voices

- Counting aloud waits for each number to finish before saying the next, so "one, two, three" is never cut short.
- Numbers are spoken all the way to one hundred.
- A wrong answer gets a gentle "Good try!" and the game counts it out together, never a scolding.
- **River** is the built-in voice. Bill, George, Sarah, Hannah and Adrian are free downloads: touch the voice button on the "Who's playing?" screen, touch a voice to download it, and touch it again to use it. Downloaded voices are kept with the saved progress, so updates don't remove them.

### Celebrations

- Celebrations grow as the numbers get closer to the top of the game: stars at first, then balloons, then confetti, then fireworks near the top.
- A new biggest number goes up one step.
- Each kind of celebration has its own cheers, so what she hears matches what she sees.
