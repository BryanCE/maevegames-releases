# Changelog

## 0.0.10

### Writing

- A tracing is judged by the path her finger took. A scribble over the letter, a filled-in letter or a ring around it no longer counts as the letter, and neither does a letter with a line left out.
- Every tracing gets a score out of 100. The green check takes a score of 70 or more, and a score of 80 or more is taken by itself a moment after she lifts her finger, unless she carries on drawing.
- A new **undo** button above the green check takes away her last line.
- A green dot marks where each line of a letter starts, at every setting.
- The pale letter turns green as she draws over it: any way round at Easy, only where she writes it the taught way at Medium, and not at all at Hard.
- A miss no longer makes the next go easier. The star shows the way again and she tries again, with nothing else changed.
- Easy, Medium and Hard, which a grown-up sets for each player, now mean:
  - **Easy**: she must draw the letter, any way round.
  - **Medium**: the letter must be neater, and writing it the way it is taught counts a little.
  - **Hard**: her line must stay on the letter and be written mostly the way it is taught.

## 0.0.9

- **Writing** is a new subject with one game, **Trace**: she draws over big and little letters with her finger, the mouse or a trackpad, then over words of two, three and four letters. A star shows how each one is written.
- A grown-up sets how closely each player must trace: Easy, Medium or Hard.
- George, Hannah and Adrian speak the writing game too; their packs update by themselves.

## 0.0.7

### For grown-ups

- A menu bar across the top, and a **Settings** window behind a password, with pages for the app, the password, players, progress, voices, updates, help and about.
- The game **updates itself**. It checks for a new version when it starts, or from Help, Check for updates, then downloads it, checks the download, swaps the program and restarts. Progress is copied first, and the check at start can be turned off.
- The Progress page opens on whoever is playing, shows what that player has, and names the player when a reset is done. **Erase everything** takes the stars too. A copy of the old progress is saved before any reset.

### For her

- A cup on each player's tile opens her trophy room.
- "Who's playing?" is asked once each time the game starts, and a menu says its question once, so coming back to a menu is quiet.
- The voice button is back on the "Who's playing?" screen, with no password.
- The bigger game sizes start past the small numbers, and a miss never takes her back down to them. Each place on the balloon journey counts higher than the one before.
- Balloons are shared with a sky friend only from five up, so both of them have something to count.

## 0.0.6

- **The map**: a winding road for each book, Ray's and the Primer, with a stop for each lesson or few lessons. A stop she has finished stays finished, and the stops finished on all the roads are her level, shown as a badge.
- **The trophy room**: cups, medals and keepsakes she has earned, in one trophy case per kind. Touching a case opens it. Trophies are earned by finishing rounds.
- **Shows**: a surprise when a level goes up, like a rainbow across the sky, falling stars or a parade of friends.
- **Grown-ups' corner**: set how quickly a player's levels move, Gentle, Steady or Stretch, or start their levels or trophies over.
- On the balloon journey, a new sky friend arrives center stage and is named aloud, and each friend is named when balloons are shared with them. Elly Elephant joins as a ninth friend.
- After a won wave she fires the rockets herself by touching the sky, with gold rockets for being right first time.

## 0.0.5

- On the balloon journey she can touch the check as soon as she has popped enough, without waiting for the rest to float away.
- In Letters, the corner speaker says the letter to find again; the big speaker in the middle is gone.
- The window and taskbar show the game's logo.

## 0.0.4

- **Reading** is a new subject, following McGuffey's Eclectic Primer, with three games:
  - **Letters**: touch a letter to hear its name and its sound, then find the letter she hears. Big letters are matched with their little letters.
  - **Words**: listen to a word from the Primer's lessons, then touch the word that says it.
  - **Slate**: touch the letters in order to spell a word, each letter making its sound.
- The reading games have their own praise, like "Great reading!", in place of the counting cheers.
- **Balloon journey**, a new math game: a hot air balloon ride through eight places, from the meadow to the moon. Each wave asks her to pop a number of balloons. Clouds hide them, the wind blows them about, some waves ask for one color or one size, and the birds are not for popping. Sky friends come along, a golden wave rewards a run of right answers, and the journey is saved for each player.
- The voices are now River, built in, and George, Hannah and Adrian as free downloads. Bill and Sarah are retired.

## 0.0.3

- **Rows**, a new math game: the balls line up in equal rows. Count the rows, then touch the number that says how many.
- In **One more bird**, more than one bird can arrive or fly away at once.

## 0.0.1

The first release of Maeve's Games.

### Games

- **Who's playing?** Each player has a picture, a color and a star total. Progress is saved per player and carries over between updates.
- **Math**, following Ray's New Primary Arithmetic, with four games:
  - **Count the balls**: touch each ball to count it, then pick how many there are.
  - **How many birds?**: birds land on a branch; count them and pick the number.
  - **Apple basket**: put the asked-for number of apples in the basket, then touch the check.
  - **One more bird**: count the birds, watch one arrive or fly away, and say how many there are now.
- Every game asks **how high to count** before it starts: up to 5, 10 or 20, and up to 100 for counting balls. Each size remembers its own level, and the numbers grow as she gets them right.
- Every game starts with a short spoken **how-to-play**.

### Counting and voices

- Counting aloud waits for each number to finish before saying the next, so "one, two, three" is never cut short.
- Numbers are spoken all the way to one hundred.
- A wrong answer gets a gentle "Good try!" and the game counts it out together, never a scolding.
- **River** is the built-in voice. Bill, George, Sarah, Hannah and Adrian are free downloads: touch the voice button on the "Who's playing?" screen, touch a voice to download it, and touch it again to use it. Downloaded voices are kept with the saved progress, so updates don't remove them.

### Celebrations

- Celebrations grow as the numbers get closer to the top of the game: stars at first, then balloons, then confetti, then fireworks near the top.
- A new biggest number goes up one step.
- Each kind of celebration has its own cheers, so what she hears matches what she sees.
